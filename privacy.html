What happens to a link?

When you scan, the full URL goes to the LinkShield backend and Google Safe Browsing. Do not submit private links, access tokens, passwords, or personal information. Google may use submitted URLs and associated data to improve its services and may share URLs, content, and metadata with third parties.

Temporary storage

The backend temporarily caches URL verdicts in memory for up to 30 minutes, subject to Google's shorter expiry. It also holds short-lived request counters for rate limiting. LinkShield has no accounts, database, or persistent scan history. Hosting providers may maintain operational request logs under their own policies.

Results have limitations

Local checks and Google's database can miss threats or flag harmless sites. A low score or no known threat match is not proof of safety. LinkShield does not visit the submitted website, inspect its content, or follow redirects. An API failure means the database check did not complete.

Advisory provided by Google. Google attribution applies to its database results, not LinkShield's independent heuristic warnings.

LinkShield is a non-commercial learning project. Google Safe Browsing cannot be used commercially without a separate agreement with Google. See the Safe Browsing terms, Google privacy policy, Netlify privacy policy, and Render privacy policy.
